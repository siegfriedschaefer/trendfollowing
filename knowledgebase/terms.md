## Trade

- Start Point
This is the price at which a position is initiated.

Long position - The price at which the buy order is executed.
Short position - The price at which the sell order is executed.

- End Point
This is the price at which a position is closed.

Long position - The price at which a sell order is executed.
Short position - The price at which a buy order is executed.


## Profit / Loss Ratio (P/L Ratio)

The P/L Ratio is a key metric that measures the profitability of a trading strategy by comparing the gains from winning trades to the losses from losing trades.

It essentially tells a trader, on average, how much profit they make for every dollar they lose.

The P/L Ratio is calculated by dividing the average profit per winning trade by the average loss per losing trade over a defined period or series of trades.P/L Ratio = (Average Profit per Winning Trade) \ (Average Loss per Losing Trade). 
To calculate this:
Calculate Total Profit: Sum the profits from all winning trades
Calculate Average Profit: Divide the total profit by the number of winning trades.
Calculate Total Loss: Sum the losses from all losing trades.
Calculate Average Loss: Divide the total loss by the number of losing trades.Calculate the Ratio: Divide the Average Profit by the Average Loss.

A P/L ratio of 3.0 (or 3:1) means that for every dollar lost on average, the trading strategy generates three dollars in profit on average.
